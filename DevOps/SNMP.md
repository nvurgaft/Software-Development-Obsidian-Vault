SNMP (Simple Network Management Protocol) is a standard protocol used to monitor, manage, and collect information from network devices such as routers, switches, servers, printers, firewalls, UPS systems, and IoT devices.

**Use cases:**

A company has several hundreds of switches, routers, server, printers and other hardware that connects to the company network.
Without SNMP, in a case of failure, an administrator would have to manually checks every component to find the culprit. Using SNMP the administrator can monitor and easily find which component failed.
